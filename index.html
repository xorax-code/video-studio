How it works

From idea to script in 4 steps

No agency. No guesswork. Just a repeatable system that takes the viral content you find and turns it into your next post.

Step 01

Find what's viral

Paste a competitor's profile or video URL. Our AI surfaces exactly what's blowing up in your niche right now.

Step 02

Analyze the hook

We break down the hook, structure, pacing, and CTA of the top-performing video so you understand why it worked.

Step 03

Rewrite in your voice

One click rewrites the script for your product, brand, and audience — original enough to stand out, proven enough to convert.

Step 04

Generate & post

Turn the script into a Veo 3 video prompt, shoot, or record voiceover — then schedule it across all your accounts from one dashboard.
