Questions

Everything you're wondering.

Do I need any video editing skills?
None. Upload a reference video or paste a script, approve the start frames, and AffiliateOS generates the clips for you.
Can I use my own face and product?
Yes. Upload a photo of your avatar and your product, and they're composited into every scene — with your background and voice too.
Which platforms can I post to?
Export vertical 9:16 for TikTok, Instagram Reels and YouTube Shorts, or 16:9 for landscape — all at 1080p.
How long does one video take?
Most clips go from reference to finished export in around 20 minutes — versus roughly an hour doing it by hand.
What AI models power it?
Google Veo 3.1 for the video generation and Gemini "Nano-Banana" for the avatar start frames.
Can I cancel anytime?
Absolutely. Start free with no card, and cancel whenever you like — no lock-in.
