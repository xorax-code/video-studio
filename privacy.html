Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 2026

1. What we collect

We collect your email address and display name when you create an account. We store your app data (accounts, scripts, projects) in Supabase, a secure cloud database. We do not sell your personal data to third parties.

2. How we use your data

Your data is used solely to provide the Affiliate OS service — syncing your settings, storing your projects, and keeping you signed in. We may use your email to send product updates or important account notices. You can opt out at any time.

3. AI processing

When you use AI features (script rewriting, scene analysis, video prompts), your content is sent to OpenAI's API for processing. This is subject to OpenAI's privacy policy. We do not store the content of AI requests beyond what is saved in your project data.

4. Cookies & local storage

We use browser local storage to cache your settings and preferences locally on your device. We do not use third-party tracking cookies or advertising pixels.

5. Data security

Your data is stored securely via Supabase with row-level security. Passwords are never stored in plain text. API keys used for AI features are stored server-side and never exposed to your browser.

6. Data deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data at any time by emailing us. We will process deletion requests within 30 days.

7. Contact

Questions? Email us at support@affiliateos.app
