Content Angles to Watch For
Affiliate / Product Review
Hook with a result, not a product
Top performers open with the outcome ("I lost 20lbs") before mentioning the product. Watch how they delay the reveal.
Health & Wellness
Before/after with a twist
The strongest accounts add a counterintuitive reason — something the viewer didn't expect caused the transformation.
Finance / Make Money
Screenshot proof in first 2s
Earnings screenshots shown immediately drive the highest scroll-stop rate. Watch how they frame the number (daily vs. monthly).
Lifestyle / Motivation
Story arc compressed into 30s
The best viral clips have a full beginning-middle-end story. Watch for the emotional pivot point — usually around the 15s mark.
Beauty / Fashion
Comparison framing wins
"Normal vs. this" or "cheap vs. expensive" structures generate curiosity loops. Note which side they show first.
Any Niche
Comment-bait endings
High-engagement accounts end with a question or a polarizing statement that forces a comment. Track how many end this way vs. a soft CTA.

In Video Studio, split your script into scenes and use AI Describe or Generate All to build prompts. Then copy to Google Flow.
